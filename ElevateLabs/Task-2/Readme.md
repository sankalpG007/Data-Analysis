# Task 2: Data Visualization & Storytelling

## Dataset Used:
Sample Superstore Sales Data

## Tools:
- Power BI

## Visuals Included:
1. Sales over time (Line Chart)
2. Profit by Category and Sub-Category (Bar Chart)
3. Sales by Region (Map)
4. Top-performing Products (Bar Chart)
5. Customer Segments (Donut Chart)
6. Summary Dashboard with KPIs

## Insights:
- The West Region has the highest sales but also the highest returns.
- Technology is the most profitable category.
- November shows peak sales, indicating holiday season influence.
